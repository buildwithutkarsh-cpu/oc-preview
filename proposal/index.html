01 / THE IDEA
What is OCd?

NOT JUST EVENTS.
A REASON TO BUILD.

OverclockedDevs is a Delhi NCR student-led hackathon organization creating spaces where young builders can turn ideas into working projects.

We bring together students, developers, designers, makers and creators around one simple idea: make something.

Our identity is built around four actions.

01 BUILD. Create something from nothing.
02 BREAK. Experiment. Challenge assumptions.
03 FORK. Take ideas somewhere unexpected.
04 FIX. Learn, iterate and ship.
