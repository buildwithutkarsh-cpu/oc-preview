01 / ABOUT

THIS ISN'T JUST ANOTHER HACKATHON ORGANIZATION.

OverclockedDevs is a Delhi NCR based student hackathon organization built around one simple idea:

give young builders a room, a deadline and a reason to make something they actually care about.

From games and websites to AI, hardware and experimental ideas, OCd creates high-energy spaces where students can learn by building.
